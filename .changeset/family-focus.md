Family focus (spec 2026-11-04). At the mid and near regimes a map with families draws leaves only for the family that dominates the screen; the others stay at the family level (fill, outline, name). Two new tuning keys, `focusFloor` (default 0.5) and `focusFull` (default 1), set the share of the largest family's on-screen items below which a family is drawn as a family and above which it shows all its leaves, rising smoothly in between. A map without families is drawn exactly as before.
