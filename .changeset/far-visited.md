A territory's fill gets fuller the more of it is visited, so the far regime shows where the reader has been: the share of its nodes with `state: "visited"` (a family's over its leaves) rounds to the nearest quarter and multiplies the fill alpha by 1 to 1.75. A spec with no visited node paints exactly as before. The boost fades out as points are drawn (the palette's points need the base fill for 3:1 contrast), and the territory button in the accessible tree adds "N of M visited".
