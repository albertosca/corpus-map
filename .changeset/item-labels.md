The near regime no longer draws a card for every item on screen. It draws compact item labels on the canvas instead: the most relevant items first (the new optional `MapNode.priority`, then the number of edges, then the id), wrapped to `tuning.itemLabelLines` lines of at most `tuning.itemLabelWidth` pixels in `theme.itemLabelFont`, placed once per spec so that none overlaps another or a territory name and none pops on pan. Clicking a label selects its item, and a point under a drawn label shows no tooltip, since the label already names it. `renderCard` now fills one card, the selected item's, shown at mid and near beside its point on the side that covers the fewest of its edges and neighbours, with a leader line when it sits farther out. Breaking: `CardContext` and the group card's "+N" badge are removed and `renderCard` receives the node only; `cardCap` now caps only the accessible tree.

`DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS.nearAt` moves from 6 to 5, so item labels arrive at a 5x zoom-in: the earliest value at which they name nearly as many of the items on screen (within 0.10) as they do at 6, in every gallery corpus on a desktop and a phone. Pass `thresholds={{ midAt: 2, nearAt: 6 }}` to keep the old behaviour. Behaviour change: thresholds built as `{ ...DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS, midAt }` with a `midAt` above 5 and up to 6, valid before, now throw a `RangeError` (`midAt` must be <= `nearAt`); set `nearAt` too. Focusing a territory from the accessible tree now lands where the item labels are fully faded in.
