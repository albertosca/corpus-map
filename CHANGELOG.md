# corpus-map

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 89f744e: Dialogue with an AI (spec 2026-11-03). New separate entry `corpus-map/assistant` with `AssistantBox` (about 2 kB gzipped): the consumer provides the AI as an async `ask` adapter, a complete answer highlights its cited items and frames them once only if the reader has not moved the view, with "Back to previous view". `CorpusMapHandle` gains `setView(view)` and `getView()`.
- 5fa9909: Add `corpus-map/filter`, an opt-in checkbox filter over any `meta` attribute. `FilterBox` reports the ids that pass through `onMatches`, to hand to `CorpusMap`'s `matches`; `facetsOf`, `idsMatching` and `intersectMatches` are the pure pieces under it, so a filter and a search compose by intersection. The main entry is unchanged.
- 93aa5c5: Organise many categories (spec 2026-10-20). Territories can carry a `group`; without one, 10 to 400 territories are grouped from their centroids (`deriveTerritoryGroups`, exported) and the far map names the groups below `tuning.groupNamesUntil` (default 1.4), cross-fading to territory names above it. The legend lists every territory (no more "+N more"), grouped, filterable, with on-screen awareness and checkboxes; `hiddenTerritories` / `defaultHiddenTerritories` / `onHiddenTerritoriesChange` dim territories instead of hiding them. A tooltip names the territories under the cursor, or under a tap on touch. Behaviour change: `onViewChange`'s `visibleCount` counts only points of territories that are not dimmed.
- d887540: Styles a consumer can define (spec 2026-11-11). `CorpusMap`'s `theme` now accepts a partial `ThemeInput` laid over `DEFAULT_THEME` (`resolveTheme` is exported); six optional line widths (`territoryOutlineWidth`, `groupOutlineWidth`, `unvisitedRingWidth`, `selectionRingWidth`, `matchRingWidth`, `neighbourRingWidth`) restyle the strokes, a bad width being a `RangeError`. New separate entry `corpus-map/theme-check` with `checkTheme(theme, { page })` (about 5 kB gzipped), the accessibility measurements the default look is held to, and a one-time development `console.warn` from `CorpusMap` when a theme fails them; production never loads it. A colour written as `#rgb`, `hsl()`/`hsla()` or a CSS colour name in a theme is converted to `#rrggbb`/`rgba()` when the theme is resolved. The core grows by about 2.3 kB gzipped (about 1.7 kB of it the colour-name table). A hand-written whole `Theme` without `territoryNameStyle` now draws upper-case names (the house default) instead of mixed case; pass `{ uppercase: false }` to keep them mixed.
- 47cb40a: Controlled view and selection: `viewport`/`defaultViewport`/`onViewportChange` (a box-independent `MapViewport { centre, zoom }`, zoom relative to the fit, with `reason` `"interaction" | "fit" | "spec" | "resize"` (`"resize"`: uncontrolled only, a box change that moves the zoom over the new fit); in controlled mode a new spec proposes its fit instead of moving the view), `selectedId`/`defaultSelectedId`/`onSelectedChange` (a ring around the selected point, `aria-current` in the accessible tree, Escape clears it before zooming out), and `onOpenTerritoryChange` (the territory under the box centre at the near regime). New optional theme token `selectionRing`. Behaviour change: `onSelect` now also fires for a press on a point, not only on a card.
- 4417c1d: `cardSizeFor(spec, options?)` (with its `CardSizeOptions`) sizes cards from the corpus's own labels: the label width at a percentile (default 0.9) split over up to `titleLines` lines (default 2), the frame clamped to `[minimumWidth, maximumWidth]` (120 to 240 px), the height holding the lines the title takes plus `extraLines`; both sides include the padding and the card frame's borders, so the renderer's own box is the padded text column. Pass the result as `cardSize`. Invalid options throw a `RangeError` naming the option, including a `padding` that leaves no text column at `maximumWidth`.
- f90b0cd: Family focus (spec 2026-11-04). At the mid and near regimes a map with families draws leaves only for the family that dominates the screen; the others stay at the family level (fill, outline, name). Two new tuning keys, `focusFloor` (default 0.5) and `focusFull` (default 1), set the share of the largest family's on-screen items below which a family is drawn as a family and above which it shows all its leaves, rising smoothly in between. A map without families is drawn exactly as before.
- a95ae75: A territory's fill gets fuller the more of it is visited, so the far regime shows where the reader has been: the share of its nodes with `state: "visited"` (a family's over its leaves) rounds to the nearest quarter and multiplies the fill alpha by 1 to 1.5. A spec with no visited node paints exactly as before. The boost fades out as points are drawn (the palette's points need the base fill for 3:1 contrast), and the territory button in the accessible tree adds "N of M visited".
- 4d09d04: Every fit (the first view, a new `spec`, `fit()`, the fit key, Back to overview) now scales and centres the map into the box less `insets`, read at the moment of the fit, so the whole map sits beside a drawer instead of under it. A fit beside an overlay reports a `ratio` below 1 (ratio 1 stays the fit of the whole box), a free area under 120 px on either axis, or one that would fit below 1/8 of the whole-box fit, is ignored, and an `insets` change alone still moves nothing.
- f67906c: New `insets` prop (`{ top?, right?, bottom?, left? }` in pixels, exported type `Insets`): the part of the map's box that a consumer overlay covers, such as a side drawer. Edge markers stay inside the uncovered area, a neighbour under the overlay counts as off screen and gets a marker, and `reveal` centres the item in the uncovered area. The fit ignores insets on purpose, so opening or closing an overlay never changes the zoom ratio or the regime. Missing, negative and non-finite sides read as 0.
- e99ef2c: The near regime no longer draws a card for every item on screen. It draws compact item labels on the canvas instead: the most relevant items first (the new optional `MapNode.priority`, then the number of edges, then the id), wrapped to `tuning.itemLabelLines` lines of at most `tuning.itemLabelWidth` pixels in `theme.itemLabelFont`, placed once per spec so that none overlaps another or a territory name and none pops on pan. Clicking a label selects its item, and a point under a drawn label shows no tooltip, since the label already names it. `renderCard` now fills one card, the selected item's, shown at mid and near beside its point on the side that covers the fewest of its edges and neighbours, with a leader line when it sits farther out. Breaking: `CardContext` and the group card's "+N" badge are removed and `renderCard` receives the node only; `cardCap` now caps only the accessible tree.
  
  `DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS.nearAt` moves from 6 to 5, so item labels arrive at a 5x zoom-in: the earliest value at which they name nearly as many of the items on screen (within 0.10) as they do at 6, in every gallery corpus on a desktop and a phone. Pass `thresholds={{ midAt: 2, nearAt: 6 }}` to keep the old behaviour. Behaviour change: thresholds built as `{ ...DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS, midAt }` with a `midAt` above 5 and up to 6, valid before, now throw a `RangeError` (`midAt` must be <= `nearAt`); set `nearAt` too. Focusing a territory from the accessible tree now lands where the item labels are fully faded in.
  
  The default themes' `cardBackground` is now opaque (`#ffffff` and `rgb(28,28,32)`, was 0.96 alpha), so nothing painted on the map, item labels included, shows through the selected card, the legend or the accessible tree.
  
  The selected card fades in and out with the points (it is hidden while they are below 0.1 opacity), and a selected point under an `insets` side gets no card. A point whose label sits off the canvas or under the selected card still gets its hover tooltip, and hovering over the selected card names nothing beneath it. `theme.itemLabelFont` may state its size in px, pt, em or rem.
- f213421: Keyboard and screen-reader access (spec 2026-10-20): the map surface is a focusable region (`label` prop, `focus()` handle) with arrow/+/-/0/Esc keys and a settled-count announcement; the accessible tree lists territories under group headings with a live "Unassigned" control and "Back to overview"; legend group headers become headings; new `headingLevel` prop (2–6; `null` from JavaScript throws like any other invalid level). A blank `label` keeps the default name. Behaviour change: territory buttons in the accessible tree are now named "Name, N items" instead of "Name (N)".
- 925e4ae: Visible keyboard focus: the accessible tree's buttons are visually hidden, so the territory (far and mid regimes) or item (near regime) whose button has keyboard focus is now outlined on the canvas — the territory's boundary rings, or a ring around the item's point — and the outline goes away on blur, or when that button leaves the tree.
  
  `Theme` gains two required fields, `focusOutline` (colour, default `#1d4ed8`, at least 3:1 against a white or `#f7f7f8` background, bare and under the default territory fill — WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11) and `focusOutlineWidth` (CSS pixels, default 3); a consumer-built `Theme` must add both, while one spread from `DEFAULT_THEME` gets them.
- 95957c5: Map feel: fit-relative level of detail with hysteresis and cross-fade, concave padded territory boundaries with collision-free names, library-owned card frames placed beside their point, hover names, and frame-coalesced dragging.
  
  Breaking: `thresholds.midAt`/`nearAt` are now multiples of the fitted scale (the zoom at which the whole corpus fits) rather than absolute scales — `DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS = { midAt: 2, nearAt: 6 }`, so every corpus now opens in the far regime. `Theme.territoryFont` is replaced by `territoryFonts: { large, medium, small }`, and `Theme` gains four more required fields — `cardBackground`, `cardBorder`, `hoverBackground`, `hoverText` — so a consumer-built `Theme` must add all four. Cards are now drawn by the library, framed and placed beside their point (with a group fallback when none fits), instead of anchored at the node's top-left corner. Selection now fires only for the primary button of a primary pointer (`event.button === 0 && event.isPrimary`); a right-click, a middle-click or a non-primary touch point no longer selects.
  
  Additions: `tuning?: Partial<Tuning>` prop and `DEFAULT_TUNING` export, holding every number the literature does not settle (wheel sensitivity, pinch gain, hysteresis, fade band, hull padding, label class cutoffs, hover radius); `cardSize?: { width; height }` prop for the card frame; a small hover tooltip showing the nearest point's name at the mid and near regimes.
- 4c8e99c: Works on a phone. Touch: two fingers pinch-zoom about their midpoint and pan together, a double tap zooms in one step about the tap, a two-finger tap zooms out one step, and a tap selects (a tap on empty map clears the selection after the double-tap window, so a double tap never clears it). Below a 600 px box the family names are drawn and fitted in a compact font, `Theme.territoryFonts.compact` (default `"700 14px system-ui, sans-serif"`), so a phone shows every family name at the fitted view; a width change across 600 swaps the names without rebuilding the scene or moving the view. When the `insets` grow over the selected item (a drawer or sheet opening on top of it), the view moves at the same zoom to bring it back into the uncovered area; a growth elsewhere, a shrink, or an item already covered moves nothing. Below a 600 px box every legend control (the toggle, Show all, group headers, rows, checkboxes and the filter) is at least 44 px tall (`LegendProps.touchTargets`), and the legend hint reads "Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to pan". The gzip size ceiling rises to 33,287 bytes (the main entry measures 33,187).
- ef11d73: Nested territories and edges (spec 2026-10-25, slice E). A territory can name a `parent` (two levels; a parent lists `members: []`, nodes belong to leaves; `parent` and `group` cannot be mixed): far views read as families, each a palette colour, and near views as the leaves, each a lighter or darker tint of its family's colour. `MapSpec.edges` (`MapEdge { source, target, weight?, reason? }`, undirected, validated) draws lines and neighbour rings for the hovered and the selected item, up to `tuning.edgesPerNode` heaviest first; off-screen neighbours of the selected item are marked at the box edge and pressing a marker goes to it; the accessible tree lists and announces the selected item's related items at mid and near. New exports: `MapEdge`, `RelatedItem`, `relatedItems(spec, id)`. New handle method `CorpusMapHandle.reveal(id)` centres an item at the current zoom without selecting it. Type changes a consumer may need to follow: `Theme` gains an optional `edge` colour (absent, lines and neighbour rings take `territoryLabel`), `Tuning` gains `edgesPerNode`, and code that implements `CorpusMapHandle` itself (a test double, say) must add `reveal`.
- ed6999b: Search results are ranked and reachable (spec 2026-11-06). `SearchBox` (in `corpus-map/search`, now about 4.1 kB gzipped) gains `rank` (`"connections"` by default: the most edges first; `"alphabetical"`; or a score function; ties always alphabetical), hands `onMatches` the ids best first, lists them under the input as an accessible combobox (the first 50, then "and N more"), and calls the new `onGo(id)` on Enter or a click on a result, never while typing. A consumer's `search` may return `{ id, score }` entries to have them sorted by score; plain ids keep the order they came in. New types `RankRule`, `RankItem`, `RankContext` and `ScoredId`. The input now has the `combobox` role instead of `textbox`.
- 5aa0e08: Search beside the map. `CorpusMap` takes a `matches` prop (a set or a list of item ids; `null` or `undefined` means no search, an empty set dims everything): the other items are dimmed and the matches ringed, composing with `hiddenTerritories`, and the item labels and the accessible tree follow. The `CorpusMapHandle` interface gains a `revealAll(ids)` member that fits the view around the matches inside `insets`; a consumer that implements that interface itself must add it. A new entry `corpus-map/search` ships `SearchBox` (debounced, a local accent-folding search over labels and `meta` unless you pass `search`, a `role="status"` count, Escape and a clear button), built separately so the main bundle does not grow for a consumer who never opts in.
- 02720f6: A more serious default look (spec 2026-11-07, "ink and paper"): a desaturated palette (eight colours per theme, re-measured by metrics 15-17), a lighter territory fill (0.16), 2.4 px points, a 1 px territory outline and a 1.75 px family outline, lighter name fonts, and upper-case spaced territory and family names. New optional `Theme.territoryNameStyle` (`{ uppercase?, spacing? }`): the transformed text and the letter spacing are what is measured and placed as well as drawn; absent or `{}` draws as before, so a custom theme without it keeps mixed-case names. The accessible tree, tooltip, legend and cards keep the real name. The default `nearAt` threshold moves from 5 to 4.5 (metric 18), and the compact family-name font from `700 14px` to `600 12px`; pass your own `thresholds` or theme to keep the old values.
- 461bf4c: `width` and `height` are now optional. Without them, `CorpusMap` measures its parent with a `ResizeObserver` (through an internal hook) and renders nothing until the first measurement; with both given, the map still fits to them immediately, with no observer. A box with zero width or height, from either source, counts as no box yet: the map paints nothing and fits to the first box whose sides are both positive. Either way, a later box change — from a new measurement, or from a later `width`/`height` prop change — keeps the current scale and keeps the world point that was at the old box's centre at the new box's centre, instead of refitting and discarding the user's zoom; a new `spec` still refits, as before. If you want to refit after a box change, pass a `ref` (typed `CorpusMapHandle`) and call its `fit()` — the same `ref` also exposes `zoomIn()`/`zoomOut()`, and a new `onViewChange` prop reports the regime, the scale ratio to the fitted scale, and the on-screen node count: once on mount, after any commit that changes one of those three values, and after every refit (a new `spec`, or `fit()`) even when they match. A new `minimap` prop (`boolean | { corner: "top-left" | "top-right" | "bottom-left" | "bottom-right" }`, default off) adds a 160 x 110 canvas in a corner of the box showing the territory shapes and the current view; pressing or dragging it centres the main view on the pressed world point, through the same coalesced `onViewChange`-firing update a drag on the map itself uses.
  
  `resolveTuning` and `resolveThresholds` are now exported from the package's own public door (they were already reachable as internal declarations, but never through `src/index.ts`). Both validate and default their input the same way `CorpusMap` already does internally, throwing a `RangeError` naming the offending field on an invalid value — useful for a consumer that wants to validate a `tuning`/`thresholds` value before handing it to `CorpusMap`, the way the demo's new hidden tuning panel does.
  
  A territory name wider or taller than the map's box is no longer drawn, since it could never be read whole there (on a phone-width box, the longest names wait for a larger one). Where a name sits never enters that decision, so a name that fits still slides off the edge clipped on pan and zoom instead of popping out.
  
  A zoom step (wheel, pinch, `zoomIn()`, `zoomOut()`) never moves opposite to its direction: when a keep-view resize has left the scale outside the zoom band (zoomed all the way in, then the box shrinks), zooming in leaves the scale where it is instead of snapping out to the band edge.
- 72644a3: Walk the map with the keyboard: while an item is selected, the arrow keys move the selection to the nearest item in that direction (spoken as its name and territory, "No item to the right" at an edge) and fire `onSelectedChange` and `onSelect`; the view follows only when the item nears the edge of the area the `insets` leave uncovered. With nothing selected, `Enter` or `Space` select the item nearest the centre, zooming in to the near regime when the points are not drawn. Behaviour change: while an item is selected the arrows no longer pan, and `Shift` + arrow pans by the small step (a tenth of the box) instead of half the box.
- 885dbdb: Visual legibility and colour. Territories get one categorical colour each (from `theme.palette`, or `Territory.colour`), painted in a single fill layer so overlaps never darken; points take their territory's colour and show visited state by shape (filled disc / ring); the first view fits territory shapes and names; new `legend` and `presentation` props; `onViewChange` reports `presentation`; new `DARK_THEME`.
  
  Migration for a custom `Theme`:
  
  | Removed | Use instead |
  | --- | --- |
  | `territoryFill` | `palette` (one opaque colour per territory) and `territoryFillAlpha` |
  | `territoryStroke` | nothing: outlines use each territory's colour |
  | `visited` | the territory colour, drawn as a filled disc |
  | `unvisited` | the territory colour, drawn as a ring |
  | (new) `pointNeutral` | colour of points and territories without a palette colour |

### Patch Changes

- 4d8b355: Fixes from the category-organisation review: the territory balloon counts "N items" (with "1 item") instead of "N papers"; territory ids containing a space no longer make two different hovers look the same; a group with a single member shows one "Show X" checkbox, not two; group names fade out before the far regime can end even when `midAt` is set low, and hold through all of far when there is no room to fade; the first view fits territory names, not group names, when it does not open in the far regime; the legend's filter box and checkboxes carry a `name` (never an `id`, so two maps can share a page), which stops Chrome flagging them as unnamed form fields.
- b9a646d: Territory outlines are stroked as one path per colour and alpha instead of one path per ring, so the arXiv demo's fitted view issues 24 outline strokes or fewer instead of 206. Where two colours' outlines cross, the colour painted later is now on top for the whole batch rather than per territory, and a translucent outline (a dimmed territory, the family cross-fade, or an `rgba` theme colour) no longer darkens where two rings of the same colour cross.
- 69e7b7f: `validateSpec` now rejects a node listed twice in one territory's `members`, which it used to accept and silently dedupe.
- b1426f1: The legend also gives up the height of the `insets` overlay on the far side of the box (a credit line or a sheet at the bottom edge for a legend in a top corner, and the mirror), so it no longer runs under it. On the side it shares with the minimap it keeps the larger of the overlay's inset and the minimap's reserve, not the sum.
- ae985ef: The legend now sits clear of the `insets` overlay on its own two sides (and gives up that height), so a consumer panel or sheet no longer covers it. In the assistant box, a touch layout gives the question field a row of its own, so the buttons wrap as a deliberate second row at 375 px.
- fe65475: Faster on a phone. A finger tap tolerates a 10 px drift before it counts as a pan (a mouse click keeps its 4 px), a finger that goes past 10 px and comes back no longer taps, the finger left after one finger of a pinch is cancelled keeps panning, a tap on empty map clears the selection only if nothing else selected an item in the double-tap window, and a tap that selects an item opens no hover balloon (the selected card already names it). Points outside the box are not drawn and the rest are drawn in one path per colour, so overlapping points now stack by colour (every point of one colour above or below every point of another) instead of in node order. The scene and the item labels are now built in time slices of a few tens of milliseconds instead of one long task: a new `spec` draws nothing (and `onViewChange` does not fire) until its scene is ready, which on a large corpus is a few hundred milliseconds after mount; a tuning or palette change on the same `spec` keeps the current scene on screen until the new one is ready, and item labels appear once placed. Scene building itself is about three times faster (territory outlines and family colours), with the same output. `relatedItems` indexes a spec once and reuses the scene's index, so selecting an item no longer walks every edge; a spec changed in place keeps its old index, so pass a new spec object after changing its edges or nodes. Switching `spec` away and straight back before the other one was built now refits, as it did before slicing. The time slicing and the batched point drawing add weight: the gzip size ceiling rises to 34,887 bytes (the main entry measures 34,787).
- dc60cad: A family of one territory named like it no longer shows its name twice during the cross-fade: the name is drawn once, as the territory's, at full alpha through the fade (while the territory's own name is not drawn yet, the family's stays). Families with a different name from their only territory are unchanged.
- b899ace: `onRegimeChange` and `onViewChange` no longer fire twice on mount under React's `<StrictMode>` in development, and `onRegimeChange` no longer re-fires when the map's box collapses to zero and comes back with the same regime. The `spec` prop's documentation now states that it must be referentially stable.
- 41dd707: `SearchBox` and `FilterBox` now call `onMatches(null)` when they unmount while holding ids, so a consumer that swaps one box for another (a layout change) is not left with the map dimmed by a box nobody can see. `FilterBox` also keeps its open panel inside the window after the window changes while it was hidden, and `facetsOf` reads a `null`, a number or a list holding `null` from `attribute` without throwing.
